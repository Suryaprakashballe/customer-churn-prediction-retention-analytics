## 📊 Power BI Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard provides an interactive view of customer churn,
risk segmentation, churn drivers, and retention prioritization.

### Dashboard Preview

![Power BI Customer Churn & Retention Dashboard](powerbi/powerbi_dashboard_preview.png)

The complete interactive Power BI report is available here:

[Open Power BI Dashboard](powerbi/customer_churn_retention_dashboard.pbix)

### Dashboard Pages

1. **Bank Customer Churn & Retention Analytics** — overall customer population, predicted churn risk, risk distribution, and retention priority.
2. **Churn Drivers & Customer Risk Patterns** — risk patterns across age, activity, geography, balance, and number of products.
3. **Retention Prioritization & Action Plan** — customer risk × value matrix, retention priorities, recommended actions, and prioritized customer records.
